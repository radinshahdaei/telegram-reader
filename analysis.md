## The application lifecycle (observed timings)

```
Waitlist → first email → second email → appointment → interview → visa
```

- **Master's cohort data:** first email ~Jul 17–20 → second email Aug 8 → interview **Aug 19** → first-to-interview ≈ **30 days**.
- **Legal (لگال) appointments:** ~3–4 weeks wait (first email Jul 16–20 → appointment Aug 4–23).
- **Visa after study interview:** usually late — cases exist of visas issued 2–3 months before semester start; work visas ~3 days. A September interview → visa ~November → **misses Winter**.

## 🧾 The prep checklist (do this now)

### 1. Join the waitlist
- TLS → `new visa` → degree category, even without admission.
- No payment to join; everything is paid in person on interview day.

### 2. Documents
- **Signed passport** (unsigned = deficiency). 15 months validity for type-D.
- **German translations**, from diploma up to your highest degree. German translations for legalization: **valid 1 year** (English: no expiry). Embassy hasn't been strict lately, but re-translate or legalize to be safe.
- **Transcript stamps:** school + regional education office + provincial office (3 stamps, each with signature).
- **Work-history letter** (no strict format; duration + duties + "employer satisfied"). Social-security records **not** required.
- **No police clearance or health certificate needed** for the study visa.
- **Motivation letter:** use the latest group form (Aug 8 version); write it yourself, don't make it look AI-generated; **every CV/motivation claim needs a supporting certificate** or leave it out. "Will you return?" → answer 50/50.
- **5–6 email exchanges with your university** dated within the last 6 months.
- **Language cert:** not older than ~1 year; if expiring, get a **نامه کفایت زبان** (letter of sufficiency) from your university — the standard workaround.

### 3. Blocked account — 12,000 EUR
- **Must be in your own name.** A relative's account or a foreign guarantor is **not** accepted (that "guarantor" offer is an agency scam).
- **EUR (not riyal).** Two bank employees + everyone who's been there: a riyal account's euro-equivalent dropping even €1 below 12,000 gets flagged as a deficiency.
- **No minimum holding time** per the embassy site — deposit days before the interview, withdraw after.
- **Banks:** Bank Melli FX branch (Simorgh, Seda-Khanda — ask for Mr. Ebrahimzadeh) or Bank Mellat FX branches. **Avoid Sepah/Ansar (military banks)** — not accepted. Daily limit ~10,000 EUR; declaration needed above 400M rials.
- Renewal year 2: a work contract (≥~992 EUR/month gross) can substitute for the full amount.

### 4. Insurance
- **Under 30 → TK** (statutory) — email **Bastian Wiesel: `bastian.wiesel@tk.de`** directly (group's known liaison) for the **M10 exemption form**.
- **Over 30 → private:** Dr. Walter **Educare24** preferred over MAWISTA. ⚠️ Auto-cancels after 3 months without bank details — set start ≈ 1 year from arrival.
- For the visa: **German "incoming" insurance** strongly recommended (Iranian travel insurance is now listed on the embassy site at min 30k EUR/3 months, but members consider it weaker).

### 5. Admission — lock it in writing NOW
- Ask your university about **لیت (late arrival)** and **دیفر (deferral)** in writing. Some universities give neither.
- **Ask about refunds BEFORE paying** — refunds usually only go to the original paying account (use a German/European account).
- Tuition watch: **FAU (Bavaria) introduces 2,000–6,000 EUR/semester from Summer 2027** (+100 EUR application fee already for Winter 2026/27); Baden-Württemberg 1,500 EUR/semester.

## ⚠️ The disputed question: "no students this winter?"

This is the load-bearing rumor right now, and it's genuinely unresolved:

- **Operators say it:** "the embassy decided not to take students," "no plan for students this winter." TLS is powerless — only the German Foreign Ministry decides.
- **The embassy's website says something softer:** only that the resumption date is *"currently unclear"* and that **previous appointments are preserved** — it never says "no students for winter."
- **History says be skeptical of the operators:** days before work appointments opened, operators also said "no plan," then started calling two days later.

**Actionable conclusion:** **plan as if Winter may not happen, but don't withdraw or cancel anything.** Your waitlist spot is your only asset, and it's explicitly preserved.
